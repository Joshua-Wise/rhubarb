# Rhubarb Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 9, 2026 (camera and sharing added for version 0.15)_

Rhubarb is an unofficial Android client for [Rex](https://superlogical.com), a terminal app for the Mac. It connects your Android device to a Rex server that you run yourself. Rhubarb is not affiliated with Superlogical.

## The short version

Rhubarb doesn't collect, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking. It talks only to the Rex servers you add, and nothing it handles passes through the developer.

## What stays on your device

Rhubarb stores a few things in its private app storage on your device:

- The addresses and names of the Rex servers you add.
- Your settings, such as theme, terminal colors and notification preferences.
- The name your device shows to the Rex server.

None of this is sent to the developer. All of it is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

## What goes to your Rex server

Rhubarb connects only to the server addresses you enter. Over that connection it sends and receives:

- Terminal input and output for the sessions you open.
- Requests to create, rename, reorder or close sessions and tabs.
- Images you choose to send to a terminal, from the photo picker, the clipboard or your keyboard.
- Your device's name, so the server can show which device is connected.

That server is yours. What happens to the data there is up to you and Rex's own policies. Rhubarb doesn't send any of it anywhere else.

## Notifications

When you turn on alerts, Rhubarb keeps a connection to your Rex server in the background and shows notifications when a session needs your attention. These notifications are created on your device from what your server reports. They don't go through Google's push service or any other third party.

## Sharing

When you share terminal output or a link, Rhubarb hands it to Android's share sheet. Where it goes from there is your choice.

When you share text or images from another app to Rhubarb, it sends them only to the tab you pick on your own Rex server.

## Permissions

- **Internet and network state:** to connect to your Rex server.
- **Notifications:** to alert you when a session needs your attention.
- **Foreground service:** to keep the connection to your server open in the background while alerts are on.
- **Camera:** only when you tap **Scan QR code** to add a server, and only if you allow it. The camera image is read on your device to find the code and is never saved or sent anywhere.

Rhubarb doesn't ask for access to your contacts, location, microphone or files. Images come only from items you pick or share yourself.

## Google Play

If you installed Rhubarb from Google Play, Google may collect crash reports and usage statistics under its own [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The developer sees only Google's anonymized summaries of these.

## Children

Rhubarb is a developer tool and is not directed at children under 13.
