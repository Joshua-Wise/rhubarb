## App name

Rhubarb for Rex

## Short description

Your Rex terminals and coding agents on Android: answer, follow and type.

## Full description

Rhubarb puts your Mac's Rex terminals on your phone. See every session, answer your coding agents when they need you, and type into any tab, wherever you are.

Rhubarb is an unofficial client for Rex, the terminal app from Superlogical. It connects to the Rex server you run on your own Mac, over Tailscale, a VPN or your local network. There's no account and nothing passes through anyone else's servers.

NOT SET UP YET? TRY THE DEMO
Tap Try the demo on the welcome screen to look around a pretend Mac with made-up sessions before you install anything.

ANSWER YOUR AGENTS
• When Claude Code or Codex asks for permission or asks a question, its options show as buttons: in the pane, on Home and on the notification.
• The screen is checked again before anything is pressed, so a stale question is never answered.
• A message box sends whole messages to the agent, with dictation and image attachments.

SEE WHAT NEEDS YOU
• Home groups agents by what they need: waiting on you, failed, finished or working.
• Swipe a card right to pin it to the top, or left to clear it or send Esc.
• Helper agents show under the agent they work with.
• Alerts when an agent needs you, and a live progress chip while one works (Android 16).

CHAT VIEW (EXPERIMENTAL)
Read a Claude Code or Codex session as a conversation instead of a terminal: your messages as bubbles, formatted replies, and commands folded into rows you can open.

A REAL TERMINAL
• Fast terminal rendering with a key bar for Esc, Ctrl, Tab and arrows.
• Find in scrollback, share output, download files from the Mac, and send photos and files to it.
• Panes can fit your phone's screen, and go back to the Mac's size when you leave.
• Controls for Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Copilot CLI and Gemini CLI: mode, model and common commands.

MADE FOR ANDROID
Material You colours, predictive back, and a side-by-side layout on tablets and foldables.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking. Rhubarb talks only to the Rex servers you add.

Rhubarb is not affiliated with Superlogical, Anthropic or OpenAI. Claude Code, Codex and other product names belong to their owners.
